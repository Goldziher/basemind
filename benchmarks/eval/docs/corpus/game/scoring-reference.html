The Quarry rule

When two or more cairns of the same colour stand on neighbouring tiles, together they form a quarry. Only the tallest cairn in a quarry scores its full height value; every other cairn in it scores 1 point regardless of height. The rule stops players from packing clusters of medium cairns into one corner of the landscape.

Worked example

Maren holds two sigils, has a cairn of height 4 and a cairn of height 3 on neighbouring tiles, and owns a ridge line of five tiles. Her two cairns form a quarry, so she scores 8 for the taller one and 1 for the other, 6 for the ridge line and 4 for her sigils: 19 points in total, < the 22 she would have scored had the cairns stood apart.
