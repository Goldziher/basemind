Planned supply interruption: Millrace trunk main replacement

The Varnholt Water Board is replacing 600 metres of cast-iron trunk main between the Millrace treatment works and Canal Street. The old main dates from 1911 and has burst four times in the last five years. While the new pipe is connected, some streets will be without water or will have low pressure.

What you should do

Fill a kettle and a few bottles before the start time. Do not run washing machines or dishwashers during the window. When supply returns, run the cold kitchen tap until the water is clear; brief discolouration is normal & harmless.

Help for vulnerable customers

Customers on the priority services register will have bottled water delivered to the door if the interruption is expected to last > 6 hours. To join the register, call the customer line or ask a neighbour to call on your behalf.

Why this work matters

Replacing the main will cut leakage, reduce bursts on the water & sewer network in the canal quarter, and let the board raise pressure to the upper floors of the new flats on Canal Street.
