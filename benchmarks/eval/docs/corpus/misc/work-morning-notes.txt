Fennick Road Community Garden: notes from the autumn work morning. These
notes are kept as a plain text file in the shared folder so anyone can
add to them without special software.

The rainwater butts by the shed were drained and scrubbed. Two of them
had cracked lids, which were replaced with the spare lids from the tool
store. The hose reel was wound in for winter.

Plot 14 has been empty since the summer. The committee agreed to offer
it to the next person on the waiting list, and to split the overgrown
blackberry bed between plots 13 and 15 if nobody takes it.

Leaf mould: the wire cage behind the compost bays is now full. Leaves
collected after this date go into the second cage, which was rebuilt
with new chicken wire and four oak stakes.

Next work morning is the first Saturday of December. Bring gloves; the
tea urn is being repaired.
