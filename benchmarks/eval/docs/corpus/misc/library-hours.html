Harrowgate Lane Library

Welcome to your neighbourhood library. Membership is free for everyone who lives, works or studies in the borough & you can join at the front desk with any proof of address.

Borrowing & renewals

Members may borrow up to 15 items for three weeks. Items can be renewed twice online or at the desk unless another member has reserved them. There are no fines for late returns, but borrowing is paused while any item is more than four weeks overdue.

Study room

The upstairs study room seats twelve and can be booked in two-hour slots, up to a week in advance. Groups of > 6 people should book the meeting room instead.

Accessibility

The building has step-free access from the car park, a lift to the first floor and a hearing loop at the front desk. Large-print and audio books are shelved on the ground floor next to the entrance.
